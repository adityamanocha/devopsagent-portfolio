**How I Think About DevOps**

DevOps is not about tools — it’s about **enabling teams to ship to production with confidence.**

Good DevOps systems:

- reduce deployment risk
- make failures predictable and recoverable
- are observable and easy to reason about
- don’t depend on heroics

My approach is pragmatic, production-focused, and shaped by real-world experience.
